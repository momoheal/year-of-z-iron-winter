A small reusable transparent 2D snow-track overlay: a short diagonal trail of six gray-dog pawprints pressed into fresh snow, alternating left and right, with subtle wind-blown powder around the edges. No dog, no human bootprints, no scenery, no text. Painterly oil-brush game art, muted blue-gray shadows, crisp readable paw shapes. True transparent RGBA background so the marks can be placed over a snow map, no solid rectangle or baked-in background. Designed as a Godot 2D overlay texture.
