A standalone directional light-beam overlay for a 2D winter game, broad cold-white searchlight cone shaped as a long tapered wedge. The source begins exactly at the top-center edge, widens faster than a narrow beam and fills most of the lower half, with soft feathered edges, a brighter center and translucent pale blue-gray spill. Entire background is true transparent alpha; no visible lamp, floor, scenery, characters, text or logos. Keep the wedge centered vertically and fully in frame, subtle painterly grain. Designed to match a 1086x1448 Godot light-cone canvas with a top-center origin.
