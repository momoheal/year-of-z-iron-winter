A standalone directional light-beam overlay for a 2D winter game, narrow warm-amber searchlight cone shaped as a long tapered wedge. The source begins exactly at the top-center edge and widens gradually toward the bottom; soft feathered edges, brighter amber center fading to transparent golden-gray edges. Entire background is true transparent alpha, no visible lamp, no floor, no scenery, no characters, no text or logos. Keep the wedge centered vertically and entirely in frame, subtle painterly texture. Designed to match a 1086x1448 Godot light-cone canvas with a top-center origin.
