A standalone directional light-beam overlay for a 2D winter game, narrow cold-white searchlight cone shaped as a long tapered wedge. The source begins exactly at the top-center edge and widens gradually toward the bottom; soft feathered borders with a brighter center and translucent pale blue-gray light. Entire background is true transparent alpha, no visible lamp, no floor, no scenery, no characters, no text or logos. Keep the wedge centered vertically and entirely in frame, painterly texture subtle and restrained. Designed to be placed under a rotating searchlight head using a top-center origin, on a 1086x1448 canvas.
